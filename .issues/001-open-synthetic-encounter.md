## What to build

Deliver the first phone-visible path into the OpenTriage MVP: opening the static application at a 390 × 844 reference viewport shows the prefilled synthetic adult chest-pain encounter, a persistent not-for-clinical-use notice, compact patient/incident context, and working Timeline and Checklist navigation based on the approved mobile direction.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Opening the app requires no login and immediately shows the synthetic encounter.
- [ ] A persistent notice states that the experience uses synthetic data and is not for clinical use.
- [ ] The patient and incident context is clearly fictional and matches the version-controlled chest-pain scenario.
- [ ] Timeline and Checklist controls switch between two phone-appropriate views without losing encounter state.
- [ ] The shell remains usable at 390 × 844 and the 360 × 800 minimum reference viewport.
- [ ] Relevant automated tests demonstrate the completed behavior.
