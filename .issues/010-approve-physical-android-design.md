## What to build

Conduct the genuine human design checkpoint on a physical Android phone using the deployed prototype. Compare the implementation with the approved timeline and checklist references and resolve whether scrolling, safe areas, keyboard behavior, terminology search, quick actions, validation recovery, and one-handed operation are ready for clinician evaluation.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A human reviewer completes the full scenario on at least one modest physical Android phone using current Chrome.
- [ ] The reviewer checks the 360 × 800 design boundary or the closest available physical viewport.
- [ ] Quick actions remain reachable, the patient header does not crowd the timeline, and the keyboard does not hide required controls.
- [ ] Medication/procedure search, validation navigation, warning acknowledgement, summary, refresh recovery, and reset are exercised.
- [ ] Findings are recorded with an explicit approve or revise decision for clinician testing.
- [ ] Any blocking findings are linked to follow-up work before this checkpoint is marked complete.
