## What to build

Run the first structured learning cycle with ambulance care providers. Dry-run the consistent chest-pain task and interview guide, facilitate hands-on sessions using the approved physical-phone build, record anonymized observations and feedback, and produce an evidence-based recommendation about whether the event-first workflow merits further investment or redesign.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The scenario prompt, observation guide, and short interview guide are documented and dry-run without coaching participants through the interface.
- [ ] Participating ambulance care providers complete the same synthetic encounter on the approved phone build.
- [ ] Facilitators record hesitation, backtracking, missed information, quick-action use, terminology search, validation recovery, and completion behavior.
- [ ] Feedback captures what feels smoother or worse than current documentation and why.
- [ ] Session notes are anonymized and stored outside the prototype without embedded analytics or recording.
- [ ] Results identify repeated strengths, repeated critical omissions or confusion, and whether providers consistently prefer the event-first workflow.
- [ ] The issue concludes with a documented proceed, redesign-and-retest, or stop recommendation; the exact quantitative investment threshold may be decided separately as agreed.
