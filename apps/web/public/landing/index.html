OpenTriageOpen-source ePCR

Patient care reporting.
Open to everyone.

OpenTriage is a browser-based electronic patient care reporting (ePCR) platform for emergency medical services, initially modeled on NEMSIS 3.5.1. It brings clinical documentation, report review, and analytics together in one workspace.
